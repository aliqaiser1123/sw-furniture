# Content Manager Guide

This guide is for marketing teams and content managers working with the Shesham Wood Furniture platform.

## 1. Homepage Banners
Navigate to **Admin -> Marketing -> Banners**.
- Banners control the primary imagery on the homepage and category pages.
- **Hero Banners**: Appear at the top of the homepage. You can set a primary headline, subtitle, and CTA link.
- **Seasonal Banners**: Used for collections (e.g., "Ramadan Sale").
- **Images**: Always upload landscape images for Hero banners and ensure they are high resolution but optimized (under 500KB is ideal). The system will automatically convert them to WebP.

## 2. Product Catalog
Navigate to **Admin -> Products**.
- **SEO Matters**: Ensure every product has a descriptive title and detailed description. The AI relies on this data to answer customer questions.
- **Tags**: Use tags (e.g., `minimalist`, `hand-carved`, `walnut-finish`) to power the search engine and recommendation algorithms.
- **Stock**: If an item is out of stock, it will still display on the site with an "Out of Stock" badge unless you change its status to `DRAFT`.

## 3. Blog & Articles
Navigate to **Admin -> Blog**.
- The blog is a crucial tool for SEO.
- Write about furniture care, interior design, and the history of Sheesham wood.
- Always include a Featured Image.

## 4. FAQs
Navigate to **Admin -> Support -> FAQs**.
- FAQs are categorized. First, create an FAQ Category (e.g., "Shipping & Delivery"), then add questions to it.
- Keep answers concise.

## 5. Static Pages
Navigate to **Admin -> Pages**.
- Use this section to manage the "About Us", "Privacy Policy", and "Terms of Service".
- Content entered here is rendered directly as a webpage on the storefront.

*Tip: The AI Assistant reads your product descriptions and FAQs. The more detailed your content, the better the AI can sell your furniture!*
